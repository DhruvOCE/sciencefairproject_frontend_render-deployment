About this project

A simple explanation of what this project does and who we are.

We're building lightweight AI models that analyze hand-drawn spirals and circles to help detect motor symptoms associated with Parkinson's and Alzheimer's disease and early cognitive changes. This project is an educational effort — we focus on data collection, model training, and trying to produce clear visualizations to help understand model behavior.

What we collect

The dataset contains scanned or photographed drawings (spirals and circles) from healthy participants and patients. These are preprocessed, then used to train simple classifiers and exploratory visual analytics. We also are trying to experiment with trying to use our alezheimers model as it only excepts csv data

Who made this

This is an educational science-fair style project created by two middle-school students. It's intended for learning and demonstration only — not for real clinical diagnosis.
